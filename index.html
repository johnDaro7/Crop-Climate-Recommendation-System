📖 How to Use CropSense

  1. Measure your conditions. Take readings of your field's temperature, humidity, and soil pH using your instruments before entering data.
  2. Use the Crop Recommender. Go to the Crop Recommender page and use the sliders to match your conditions. The system gives you an instant recommendation.
  3. Save your result. After getting a recommendation, click "Save This Reading" to log it to your history for future reference.
  4. Log raw data separately. Use the Log Climate Data page to record detailed readings — including rainfall, field name, and personal notes — even without needing a recommendation.
  5. Review your history. Go to History to see your saved readings displayed as cards with all measurements visible.
  6. Analyse trends. Visit the Analytics page for visual charts of your temperature, humidity, pH, and crop distribution over time.

⚙️ Recommendation Logic

The backend engine uses these rules to determine your crop recommendation:

ConditionResult
Temp >20°C AND Humidity >50% AND pH >6.0🌾 Rice
Temp >25°C AND Humidity <40%🌽 Maize
All other conditions🌾 Wheat

🌍 About CropSense

CropSense is a climate-intelligent crop recommendation platform built to help Nigerian farmers and agronomists make data-driven planting decisions.

It combines real-time climate logging with backend analysis to deliver personalised recommendations based on temperature, humidity, and soil pH.

Stack: Node.js · Express · MongoDB Atlas · Vanilla JS
